When would you bring us in?

  • Your strongest investigator is already carrying more than they should.
  • A vacancy may take months to fill.
  • Your new leader is still getting oriented.
  • The allegation involves someone in the team's reporting line.
  • Or the case is sensitive enough that the choice of investigator will be scrutinized just as closely as the findings.

Sometimes you need more capacity. Sometimes you need distance from the people involved. Sometimes you need both.

And yes, you may need another full-time hire. We understand that. But hiring and onboarding take time, and the complaint on your desk still needs to be handled now.
